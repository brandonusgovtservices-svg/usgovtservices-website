Why Team With Us

The SDVOSB-to-SDVOSB advantage.

Similarly Situated Entity

SDVOSB-to-SDVOSB subcontracting qualifies as a "Similarly Situated Entity" handoff — it does not count against your Limitations on Subcontracting.

No New Vetting Burden

We hold the same SDVOSB standard you already meet. No new vendor-qualification paperwork for your contracting officer or program office.

Extended Reach, No Overhead

Extends your firm's geographic reach without adding headcount, per diem costs, or new vendor onboarding to your prime contract.

Shared VA Past Performance

Both firms build VA past performance on every task order — positioning both for larger, multi-site federal awards over time.
