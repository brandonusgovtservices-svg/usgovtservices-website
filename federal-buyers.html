Federal Socioeconomic Designations

How we qualify for set-asides

Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB)

US Government Services LLC is a 100% veteran-owned small business and a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business certified by SBA under 13 C.F.R. Part 128. Our managing member, LTC John Michael Stacy, USA (Ret.), holds a 100% permanently and totally service-connected disability rating from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

Per SBA's 2024 final rule, only firms certified by SBA VetCert may be awarded SDVOSB set-aside or sole-source contracts. Verify our certification at veterans.certify.sba.gov.

Small Business

US Government Services LLC qualifies as a small business under the SBA size standards for each of our registered NAICS codes.

VA Veterans First Eligible

As a certified SDVOSB, US Government Services LLC is eligible for VA "Veterans First" priority under 38 U.S.C. § 8127, including sole-source services awards up to $5 million per VAAR 819.7008.
