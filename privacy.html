Privacy Policy

How US Government Services LLC handles your information.

Effective date: 2026.

Information we collect

This website collects only the information you choose to share with us — primarily through email (John@usgovtservices.com) or telephone ((703) 672-1844). We do not require account creation, and we do not gate downloads behind email forms.

How we use information

Information you share with us by email or phone is used solely to respond to your inquiry, evaluate potential contracting or teaming opportunities, and maintain professional records of our correspondence. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies & analytics

This website does not deploy non-essential cookies or third-party analytics scripts by default. If we add web analytics in the future, we will update this policy and provide a cookie notice in accordance with applicable law.

Server logs

Our hosting provider may automatically record standard server log information (IP address, browser type, referring page, timestamp) for security, troubleshooting, and operational purposes. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

Federal-contract data

Information you provide in connection with a federal procurement or contract is governed by the applicable contract terms and federal regulations, including the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) and any agency-specific rules. Procurement-sensitive information is handled accordingly.

Security

We follow reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure; if you have particularly sensitive information, please contact us by phone.

Your choices

You may request a copy of information we have on file for you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (subject to records-retention requirements). Send requests to John@usgovtservices.com.

Children

This website is intended for business and federal-government audiences. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date at the top of this page.
